ThreadPort — Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 31, 2026

The short version

ThreadPort does not collect, transmit, remotely store, sell, or share any of your data. Everything the extension does happens locally in your browser. ThreadPort has no servers.

What ThreadPort processes, and where

  • Conversation text. When you click a transfer button, ThreadPort reads the messages of the AI conversation visible in your current tab and writes them into the message box of the destination AI site you chose. This text is held briefly in your browser's local extension storage (chrome.storage.local) during the handoff and is deleted immediately after it is inserted. It is never sent to us or to any third party.
  • Usage counter. A number (how many transfers you made this month) is stored in your browser's extension storage (chrome.storage.sync) to enforce the free plan limit. It contains no conversation content.
  • Settings. Your preferences (e.g. auto-send on/off) are stored the same way.

What ThreadPort does NOT do

  • No analytics, no tracking pixels, no telemetry
  • No accounts, no emails collected
  • No cookies
  • No data leaves your machine except the text you chose to insert into the destination AI site — which you then review and send (or not) yourself

Permissions explained

  • storage — save your settings and the monthly usage counter
  • Access to chatgpt.com, claude.ai, gemini.google.com — read the conversation on the source site and insert it on the destination site. ThreadPort only runs on these three domains.

Payments (Pro plan)

Payments are processed by a third-party payment provider; ThreadPort never sees or stores your payment details. The payment provider's privacy policy applies to the checkout process.
