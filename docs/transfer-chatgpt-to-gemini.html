How to Transfer a ChatGPT Conversation to Gemini

Updated August 2026 · 5 min read

Gemini is strong at long documents, search-grounded answers and anything touching the Google ecosystem — so it's a natural place to continue a conversation that started on ChatGPT. The catch: there's no official bridge between the two, and Google's own import feature has a major regional gap.

Here are the three ways to do it, honestly compared.

Method 1: Copy-paste with a handoff prompt (free, manual)

Pasting a raw wall of text into Gemini gets you a summary of your own conversation — usually not what you want. Framing it as a continuation fixes that:

You are continuing a conversation that started on ChatGPT. Below is the
full transcript. Do not summarize it back to me — just pick up where it
left off, keeping the same context, decisions and tone.

=== TRANSCRIPT START ===
[paste your conversation here]
=== TRANSCRIPT END ===

The manual method's friction points:

  • UI junk comes along. Selecting a ChatGPT thread drags in button labels, citations and widget content that you'll delete by hand.
  • Label the speakers ([User] / [Assistant]) or Gemini can conflate who said what.
  • Long chats need pruning — keep the first message (the task) and the recent tail.

Method 2: Gemini's native import — with a big caveat

Since March 2026, the Gemini app can import conversation history from other AI assistants: you export your ChatGPT data as a ZIP archive and upload it. Two things to know before you plan around it:

  • It's not available in the EEA, UK or Switzerland. If you're in Europe, the option simply doesn't appear.
  • It's a bulk, one-time migration. It ingests your archive so Gemini knows your history — it's not designed for "continue this specific conversation right now", and the export-ZIP-upload loop is slow if that's all you wanted.

Good for: permanently switching to Gemini with your history, outside Europe.
Bad for: EU users (unavailable), and anyone who just wants to continue today's conversation.

Frequently asked questions

Why doesn't Gemini's import work in the EU?

Google hasn't stated a reason publicly; the feature launched without EEA, UK and Switzerland availability, most likely for regulatory review reasons. Until that changes, European users need one of the other two methods.

Does Gemini understand a pasted ChatGPT conversation?

Yes — with the handoff framing, Gemini picks up mid-task and responds to the last open question. Without the framing, it tends to summarize the transcript instead of continuing it.

Can I also go the other way, from Gemini to ChatGPT?

Manually, yes — same technique. Gemini's own copy behavior adds hidden accessibility labels ("You said…") that you'll want to clean. ThreadPort handles the Gemini → ChatGPT direction (and Gemini → Claude) automatically.
