How to Transfer a Gemini Conversation to ChatGPT

Updated August 2026 · 5 min read

Maybe you started a task in Gemini and want ChatGPT's take, hit a limit, or simply prefer continuing there. Whatever the reason, there's no official bridge in this direction: Google added an import feature to Gemini, but nothing to move a conversation out to ChatGPT, and ChatGPT has no import feature at all.

Here are the three ways that actually work, honestly compared.

Method 1: Copy-paste with a handoff prompt (free, manual)

Pasting a raw transcript into ChatGPT usually gets you a polite summary of your own conversation. Framing it as a continuation fixes that:

You are continuing a conversation that started on Google Gemini. Below is
the full transcript. Do not summarize it back to me — just pick up where
it left off, keeping the same context, decisions and tone.

=== TRANSCRIPT START ===
[paste your conversation here]
=== TRANSCRIPT END ===

The friction is on the Gemini side, and it's real:

  • No "copy whole conversation" button. Gemini's copy icon copies one response at a time. For a long thread that's a lot of clicking, and your own prompts aren't included — you have to select and copy those separately.
  • Selecting the whole page drags in junk. If you select-all instead, hidden accessibility labels ("You said…", "Gemini said…"), button captions and suggestion chips come along, and you'll be cleaning them by hand.
  • Label the speakers ([User] / [Assistant]) or ChatGPT can conflate who said what.
  • Very long chats need pruning — keep the first message (the task) and the recent tail.

Method 2: Export through Google Docs (free, partial)

Under each Gemini response there's a Share & export option that can send that response to a Google Doc. From there you can copy clean text into ChatGPT.

Two limitations to know before you rely on it:

  • It exports responses, not the conversation. Your own prompts — the half that gives the thread its meaning — aren't part of the export, so you're rebuilding the dialogue by hand anyway.
  • It's one response at a time. For a ten-turn conversation, that's ten exports.

Good for: carrying over one long, important answer (a report, a plan) in clean formatting.
Bad for: continuing an actual back-and-forth conversation.

Google Takeout can export your entire Gemini history in bulk, but it produces an archive for your records — not something ChatGPT can ingest. See our guide: How to export a Gemini conversation.

Frequently asked questions

Can ChatGPT import my Gemini history directly?

No. As of August 2026, ChatGPT has no import feature for other assistants' conversations. The transfer always goes through text — pasted manually or by an extension.

Does ChatGPT understand a pasted Gemini conversation?

Yes — with the handoff framing, ChatGPT picks up mid-task and responds to the last open question. Without the framing, it tends to summarize the transcript instead of continuing it.

Why does my copied Gemini text say "You said" everywhere?

Those are hidden accessibility labels Gemini adds for screen readers. They become visible when you select and copy the page. Delete them, or use a tool that strips them automatically.

Can I go the other way, from ChatGPT into Gemini?

Yes — that direction even has a native option for bulk migration (rolling out in Europe during 2026). See our guide: How to transfer a ChatGPT conversation to Gemini.
