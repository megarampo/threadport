## Long description
Ever wanted a second opinion from Claude on a ChatGPT conversation? Or to continue a Gemini chat in ChatGPT without copy-pasting twenty messages?

ThreadPort moves your AI conversations between ChatGPT, Claude and Gemini in one click — with the full context, cleanly formatted, ready to continue.

HOW IT WORKS
1. Open any conversation on ChatGPT, Claude or Gemini
2. Click ThreadPort and pick the destination AI
3. A new chat opens with your whole conversation loaded as context — review it and hit send

WHAT MAKES IT CLEAN
• Smart extraction: strips buttons, widgets, citations and UI noise — only the real messages travel
• Long chats are trimmed intelligently (keeps the task definition and the recent messages)
• Nothing is sent automatically unless you enable it — you always review first

PRIVATE BY DESIGN
Everything runs in your browser. Your conversations never touch our servers — we don't have servers. No account, no tracking, no analytics.

FREE PLAN
10 transfers per month, all features included. Pro unlocks unlimited transfers.

WHY PEOPLE USE IT
• Get a second opinion from another AI mid-conversation
• Each AI is good at different things — draft with one, refine with another
• Escape rate limits: hit a cap on one AI, continue on another
• Compare answers to the same conversation across models

Works with chatgpt.com, claude.ai and gemini.google.com.
